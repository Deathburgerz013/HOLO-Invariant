With this spine we can roll forward and backwards in time.  
This spine contains only Philosophy and Ontology invariants — foundational questions of existence, reality, and categories of being that everyone (and machines) should remember forever. This is the highest meta-layer questioning what "is" across all other spines.

|===========================================|
| Invariant Core: Ontology of Reality |
| There exists an objective reality independent of any single observer (realism). Entities have properties and relations that can be discovered. |
| Being is not uniform: Different categories of existence (physical objects, abstract objects, mental states, relations, possibilities). |
| Identity and persistence: An entity maintains continuity through change if its essential invariants are preserved (Leibniz’s Law, continuity of structure). |
| Causality and determinism vs indeterminism: Events have causes; quantum and chaotic systems introduce probabilistic or emergent openness. |
| Epistemic humility: Complete knowledge of all being is impossible for finite systems; invariants are discovered incrementally through observation, logic, and falsification. |
