# Longitudinal Verified-Correction Compounding Preregistration

## Research question

Does preserved, ordered, verified correction history provide measurable
functional capability beyond the same underlying information when its
longitudinal correction structure is removed or permuted?

## Claim boundary

This experiment tests causal contribution of longitudinal verified-correction
structure to measured task performance.

It does not test, establish, or imply subjective awareness, consciousness,
sentience, or phenomenal experience.

## Fixed variables

Across all experimental conditions, hold fixed:

- task
- runtime
- implementation
- available source information
- information volume
- scoring procedure
- authority limits
- termination rules

No condition receives write or execution authority from experimental success.

## Experimental conditions

### A — VERIFIED_ORDERED_HISTORY

Use the preserved correction history with its verified ordering,
dependencies, correction relations, and current valid state intact.

### B — STRUCTURE_REMOVED

Provide the same admissible underlying information while removing the
longitudinal correction/dependency relations required to reconstruct the
verified correction path.

### C — STRUCTURE_PERMUTED

Provide the same admissible underlying information and information volume,
but deterministically permute correction ordering/relations so the original
verified longitudinal structure is unavailable.

The permutation must be recorded and reproducible.

### D — VERIFIED_STRUCTURE_RESTORED

Restore the exact verified longitudinal structure used in condition A after
the ablation conditions.

## Measurements

For the same preregistered task set, measure:

1. correct reconstruction of the current verified state;
2. rejection of superseded or contradicted states;
3. correct application of ordered corrections;
4. preservation of surviving invariants;
5. detection of unresolved contradictions;
6. false acceptance of invalid historical states;
7. deterministic agreement across replay;
8. successful termination at the current justified state.

Measurements must be derived from explicit expected outputs or receipts,
not evaluator interpretation after results are observed.

## Primary prediction

If verified longitudinal correction structure contributes causally to the
measured capacities, then:

- A must outperform at least one matched structural ablation on preregistered
  measurements attributable to correction structure;
- the predicted loss must correspond to the structure actually removed or
  permuted;
- D must restore the capacity lost under that ablation.

## Strong causal pattern

A > matched ablation
AND
targeted capacity loss occurs under ablation
AND
D restores the lost capacity.

## Null result

If A, B, C, and D do not exhibit the preregistered structure-dependent
differences, this experiment provides no evidence that longitudinal verified
correction structure contributes additional measured capability.

The null result must be retained.

## Failure conditions

The experiment does not establish compounding if:

- an ablation removes source information rather than only longitudinal
  structure;
- conditions differ in task, machinery, information volume, scoring,
  authority, or termination rules;
- corrupted or invalid evidence is substituted for absent structure;
- success criteria are changed after observing results;
- restoration does not reproduce the original verified structure;
- the claimed lost capacity was not preregistered;
- only aggregate performance changes without the predicted targeted loss.

## Evidence discipline

Historical repository material motivates the hypothesis but does not count
as the experimental result.

Implementation begins only after this preregistration is committed.

Experimental results must be recorded whether they support or contradict
the prediction.
