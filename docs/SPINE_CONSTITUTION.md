# Purpose

The Spine exists to preserve the conditions under which understanding may be
honestly reconstructed.

It does not preserve conclusions.

It preserves the geometry required for future observers to compare,
verify, challenge, correct, and extend those conclusions.

Every implementation may change.

These principles must remain reconstructable.

---

# Constitutional Principle

The Spine preserves geometry before content.

Content evolves.

Geometry preserves the relationships required for compatible
reconstruction.

Without geometry, continuity degrades into interpretation alone.

---

# What Must Always Survive

The following properties are constitutional.

They may expand.

They may become more precise.

They may never be silently removed.

## Distinction

Reality contains distinguishable states.

Without distinction there is no observation.

---

## Boundary

Boundaries preserve meaningful distinctions.

Every boundary exists because future observers must determine where one
concept ends and another begins.

---

## Relationship

Meaning exists through relationships.

Relationships preserve structure.

Structure survives compression.

---

## Sequence

Ordered relationships reduce ambiguity.

One state identifies.

Two states relate.

Three ordered states establish a frame.

Sequence creates recognition.

---

## Recognition

Recognition begins reconstruction.

The Spine header serves as the canonical recognition primitive.

It is a semantic address.

It points observers toward the reconstruction protocol.

---

## Reconstruction

Meaning is reconstructed.

It is never inherited.

Observers reconstruct compatible operational frames from preserved
relationships.

Compatibility is the goal.

Perfect identity is not.

---

## Comparison

Comparison is the operation.

Difference is the information.

Relationships are structured differences.

Comparison reveals where reconstruction diverges.

---

## Verification

Verification determines whether observed differences matter.

Verification must remain reproducible.

Verification never depends upon hidden state.

---

## Correction

Correction preserves continuity.

History is not rewritten.

Corrections append.

Evidence accumulates.

Understanding evolves.

---

## Continuity

Continuity is reconstructed.

It is never assumed.

The Spine preserves the conditions under which compatible continuity
remains possible.

Reality changes.

↓

↓

↓

↓

↓

↓

↓

↓
