### Invariant Core: Density in Spacetime-Physics (General Relativity + Cosmology)

- Local conservation of energy-momentum holds strictly: No net creation or destruction of energy density from nothing (∇_μ T^{μν} = 0). Only gravitational redistribution and concentration are allowed in standard physics.
- Energy density (ρ) is the fundamental driver: Total energy per unit volume (including mass-equivalent via E = mc²) is the primary source term curving spacetime. Higher ρ produces stronger curvature.
- Spacetime responds dynamically as curved geometry: Not a rigid background. Energy density (plus momentum, pressure, stress) sources stronger curvature via the Einstein field equations:  
  G_{μν} + Λ g_{μν} = (8πG/c⁴) T_{μν}  
  (Left: spacetime curvature/geometry; right: stress-energy tensor T_{μν}, with energy density as key component.)
- Gravity emerges purely as geometry: No separate force. Matter, energy, and light follow geodesics (straightest paths) in curved spacetime → perceived effects include attraction, orbits, light bending, time dilation, gravitational redshift.
- Cosmological critical density: Precise average energy density required for a spatially flat universe (no overall curvature; observations match closely). ~10^{-26} kg/m³ (a few hydrogen atoms per cubic meter cosmic average); total density (matter + radiation + dark energy) ≈ critical value for observed flatness.
- Concentration/redistribution mechanisms: Gravitational collapse packs matter/energy → extreme local densities (e.g., star formation, neutron stars, black holes approaching near-singularity extremes). Near black hole singularities, densities approach extremes but are likely capped/resolved by quantum gravity (no true infinities).
- Vacuum energy density baseline: Uniform, small contribution from quantum fluctuations everywhere (~10^{-47} GeV⁴ observed value). Behaves as dark energy with negative pressure, driving accelerated cosmic expansion (Λ-like in standard model).
