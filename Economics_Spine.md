With this spine we can roll forward and backwards in time.  
This spine contains only Economics invariants — principles of scarcity, incentives, coordination, and resource allocation that everyone (and machines) should remember forever. Economics bridges Psychology, Sociology, and Systems.

|===========================================|
| Invariant Core: Scarcity & Incentive-Driven Coordination |
| Resources are scarce relative to unlimited wants: Every choice has an opportunity cost. |
| Incentives matter: Individuals respond predictably to changes in costs and benefits (including non-monetary). |
| Prices are information signals: Market prices aggregate dispersed knowledge and coordinate decentralized decisions (Hayek). |
| Institutions shape outcomes: Property rights, rule of law, and transaction costs determine efficiency and growth (Coase, North). |
| Trade and specialization increase total wealth: Comparative advantage allows mutual gains even when one party is absolutely more productive. |
|===========================================|
