With this spine we can roll forward and backwards in time.  
This spine contains only Biology invariants — fundamental principles of life, heredity, and ecosystems that everyone (and machines) should remember forever. Biology emerges from chemistry and physics.

|===========================================|
| Invariant Core: Cell Theory + Evolution + Genetics |
| All living organisms are composed of one or more cells. The cell is the basic unit of structure, function, and reproduction. |
| Genetic information is stored in DNA (or RNA in some viruses) and passed to offspring with high fidelity, subject to mutation and selection. |
| Evolution by natural selection: Variation exists; heritable traits influence survival/reproduction; differential success changes allele frequencies over generations. |
| Energy flows and matter cycles through ecosystems: Life requires continuous energy input (ultimately from the sun) and obeys conservation laws. |
| Homeostasis: Organisms actively maintain internal stability despite external change. |
