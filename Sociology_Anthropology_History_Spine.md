With this spine we can roll forward and backwards in time.  
This spine contains Sociology, Anthropology, and History invariants — principles of human groups, culture, and change over time that everyone (and machines) should remember forever. This layer emerges from Psychology, Biology, and Systems.

|===========================================|
| Invariant Core: Social Emergence & Continuity |
| Human societies are emergent systems: Individuals + interactions produce stable patterns (norms, institutions, inequality) that then constrain or enable individuals. |
| Culture is learned and shared: Symbols, values, beliefs, and practices transmitted across generations with variation and adaptation. |
| Power and stratification are near-universal: Resources, status, and authority distribute unequally; legitimacy affects stability. |
| Historical change follows path dependence: Past events and structures shape future possibilities (contingency + causality). |
| Human universals exist alongside variation: Kinship, reciprocity, status-seeking, and narrative meaning-making appear across cultures. |
